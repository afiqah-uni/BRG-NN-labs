# Topic 6: Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) in Cloud Infrastructure

## Comparing On-Premises and Cloud Infrastructure

In this activity, I compared the estimated Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) of on-premises infrastructure with cloud services from AWS and Microsoft Azure over a three-year period.

I organised the costs into categories such as hardware, software, storage, networking and maintenance. For the on-premises option, I considered the initial purchase of a physical server and other equipment, together with ongoing operating expenses. For AWS and Azure, I considered the estimated costs of virtual machines, storage and networking.

I used Microsoft Excel to organise the cost information and calculate the estimated monthly, annual and three-year totals. This helped me compare the financial differences between maintaining physical infrastructure and using cloud-based services.
